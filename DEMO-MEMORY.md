# Nib · Memory: 60-second demo

**Before judging:** open https://nibreads.vercel.app/memory/ on the iPhone, volume up. Leave it on the "Tap anywhere to start" screen. Do one live room at the venue beforehand to warm everything up (about $0.20).

| Time | Manraj says / does | Judges see / hear |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | "Some rooms only live in memory now: a grandmother's kitchen, a house that's gone. If you're blind there isn't even a photo. Nib's Memory mode lets you walk back in, just by talking." | The start screen |
| 8–15 s | Tap the screen. Nib says *"Memory. Tell me about a room you remember. Just start talking."* Then say: **"My grandmother's kitchen in Punjab, on a winter evening. She's making rotis on the tawa, the radio is playing old songs, and there's a neem tree outside the window."** | Your words appear. Nib: *"Got it. Building your room."* "Building the room… 12 s" with steps ticking off |
| 15–35 s | While it builds: "Nib never asks you to fill in a form. It reads the clues (Punjab, rotis, a neem tree) and builds the room the way that home really looked. Anything it adds, it labels." | At ~22 s the room opens. Nib says what's where: *"In front of you… on your right… behind you…"* Lift the phone and turn: it's 360°, and the radio plays from where the radio is. |
| 35–48 s | **"Hey Nib, show what's real."** | Yellow tags = from your words; dashed tags = imagined by Nib. Nib reads both lists aloud. |
| 48–60 s | **"Hey Nib, make it a summer afternoon."** "Every change reaches the whole room, not one spot." | *"Changing the whole room."* It finishes ~25 s later, during Q&A: the light changes everywhere, behind you too. |

**If the Wi-Fi is slow or anything fails:** say **"Hey Nib, try the example"** (or tap *Try the example*). The same Punjab kitchen opens instantly with its spoken description. It's pre-built and needs no network for the models.

**Tips**
- Start every command with "Hey Nib". Don't say "Nib" casually during the pitch.
- Hold the phone 20–30 cm from your mouth. The room is noisy.
- The ⟳ button: look around by moving the phone. The ♪ button: room sound.
- To start again, say "Hey Nib, start over".

**Q&A lines (honest)**
- *Is it accurate?* "It's an imagined reconstruction, not a scan. The labels show exactly which parts came from your words and which Nib imagined. You can correct anything by voice."
- *Hasn't this been done?* "Photo-to-3D worlds exist: World Labs Marble, Genie 3. Ours needs no photo, works by voice for blind people, tells you what's where, and never passes off an invention as yours."
- *How fast?* "About 22 seconds to step inside and about 25 for the full 360, on today's event key."
- *Cost?* "About 15–20 cents a room."
